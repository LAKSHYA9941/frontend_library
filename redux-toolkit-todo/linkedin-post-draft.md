Today, our mentors at Sheryians Coding School assigned us a challenge unlike any other.
No lectures. No complete roadmap. Just a couple of resources and one mission:
Explore, understand, build, and teach it in your own way — Redux Toolkit.

Honestly, it felt intimidating at first since I hadn't touched Redux before. But as developers, nobody's going to teach us every technology we'll ever use — reading docs, experimenting, breaking things, and figuring it out is the actual job. So instead of overthinking it, I just started.

Over the last few hours I read through the Redux Toolkit docs, watched a reference video, built a small Todo app from scratch, and wrote up my own documentation explaining everything in my own words.

Here's what I learned 👇
🔹 What Redux Toolkit is and why it exists (goodbye boilerplate)
🔹 configureStore() and the idea of a single global store
🔹 createSlice() — bundling state, reducers, and actions together
🔹 Actions & Reducers, and how Immer lets you write "mutating" code safely
🔹 useSelector & useDispatch — reading from and writing to the store from React
🔹 The one-way data flow: UI → dispatch → reducer → new state → UI
